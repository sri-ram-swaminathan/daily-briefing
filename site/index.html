Our promise

Everyone reads the same news.
No one reads it the same way.

The News Council reads every story through the eyes of seven very different people, picks out what matters to you, and lets you talk back. Ask for sources, dig deeper, skip ahead, go back, and it remembers where you left off.

I. Nuanced

A baker, an epidemiologist, a delivery rider, a retired teacher, an organizer, a former journalist and a philosopher read the same story and notice different things. Stepping into someone else's shoes shows you angles you would have scrolled past.

      Still under construction

      The prototype works. Three tracks of work come next: who pays for it, how it scales, and how it stays fair.

      I · Business

      Find the people who pay

      The council's cost grows with the news, not the audience, so every extra listener improves the margin. The job now is signing the first paying customers.

      • Publishers offer it as a premium subscription tier on top of the journalism they already own.
      • Professional desks (finance, policy, comms) pay per seat for a briefing tuned to their beat.
      • Syndicators like Newstex resell it: one integration, many outlets.

      Target first paying pilots with a publisher and a syndicator

      II · Technical

      Retrieval that scales

      Today the archive is small enough to read directly. That stops working once we cover many more sources and scrape them all day.

      • RAG at scale: a vector database with hybrid keyword and semantic search, so the council pulls the right handful of articles out of millions.
      • Continuous sourcing: a scraping and ingestion pipeline that runs around the clock, deduplicates stories and keeps the index fresh.
      • Many more databases: wires, archives and public records, not just today's few feeds.
      • Unorthodox local sources: Instagram, TikTok and community channels, where local news often breaks first.
      • Source validation: every claim checked against the source it cites, with unverified posts flagged or dropped.

      Target millions of articles, every citation verified

      III · Ethics

      Fair personas, private data

      A council that speaks for people has to represent them fairly and treat what they tell it with care.

      • No stereotypes: co-write personas with the people they stand for and audit them for racist or stereotyped portrayals before they go live.
      • GDPR by design: collect only what we need, with clear consent, data kept in the EU, and the right to see or delete it.
      • No hidden monitoring: listener signals are logged only with consent, anonymised, and never sold or used to track people.

      Target audited personas and GDPR-compliant data
