# What News Council costs

Generated by `tools/cost_model.py` from the real files. Prices are assumptions listed at the bottom; edit
`PRICES` and rerun.

## Headline numbers

| Scenario | Articles | Council (as built) | Council (optimised) | Readers | Per reader | Total (as built) | Total (optimised) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Full dataset, once | 1,740 | $12.84 | $4.81 | 2 | $0.475 | $13.79 | $5.76 |
| 10x the dataset | 17,400 | $128.37 | $48.10 | 20 | $0.475 | $137.87 | $57.60 |
| One newsroom day (185 articles), 1,000 readers | 185 | $1.36 | $0.51 | 1,000 | $0.475 | $476.17 | $475.32 |
| 10x newsroom day, 10,000 readers | 1,850 | $13.65 | $5.11 | 10,000 | $0.475 | $4,761.73 | $4,753.19 |

* **Council** reviews each article once, with seven personas and a moderator on `gpt-4.1-mini`
  (~1,388 tokens of article text each). It's shared by every reader.
* **Optimised** adds a `gpt-4.1-nano` triage that skips articles no persona would react to (about 45%:
  price tickers, duplicated translations), and puts the article first in the prompt so six of seven
  persona calls hit OpenAI's prompt cache.
* **Per reader**: picking 6 stories from 40 prefiltered candidates ($0.020),
  narrating about 2,800 characters with ElevenLabs ($0.282, or
  $0.042 with OpenAI TTS), and three spoken follow-up questions with web search
  ($0.172).
* **Short video** (optional): one 24-second reel costs about $2.42 (Sora 2 at
  $0.10/second). Make a few per day for the top stories, not one per reader.

Cost grows linearly: 10x the articles is 10x the council cost; 10x the readers is 10x the reader cost. Voice is
the largest per-reader line, so the budget tier swaps ElevenLabs for OpenAI TTS on free accounts.
